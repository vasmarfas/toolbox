# Apartment Lease No. 17
City ____________|Date ____________
The Landlord and the Tenant have agreed as follows.
## 1. Subject
The Landlord lets the apartment to the Tenant to live in,
and the Tenant pays the rent on time and keeps the property
in good order.
## 2. Rent and payment
The rent is paid monthly by the 5th day of the month.
The Tenant pays for utilities by the meter readings
and keeps the receipts.
## 3. Term
The lease runs for 11 months and may be extended
by agreement of the parties.
## 4. Duties of the parties
The Tenant does not sublet the apartment and reports
any faults to the Landlord within three days.
